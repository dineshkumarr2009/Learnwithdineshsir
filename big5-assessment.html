  • 120 statements — rate how accurately each describes you, roughly 15–20 minutes
  • Instrument: IPIP-NEO-120 (Johnson, 2014) — a validated, public-domain measure of the Five-Factor Model, built from the International Personality Item Pool
  • You'll get: 5 domain scores + 30 facet scores, with plain-language interpretation and workplace relevance
  • Be honest, not aspirational — answer as you actually are, not as you'd like to be. There are no right or wrong answers.

This is a self-report screening tool for classroom learning, not a clinical or diagnostic instrument. Nothing you enter is sent anywhere — it stays in this browser tab.
