Wording help, one sentence at a time

Paste a sentence or two you've written (up to 80 words). You'll get two alternatives in academic register that keep your meaning and your claims. Nothing is added that only you can supply: where evidence or a source is needed, you'll see a placeholder such as [Author, Year] to fill in yourself. You can also choose Suggest wording on any flagged sentence in the other tabs.
